# Research Papers and References

The following research papers were referred to during the development of our project.

## 1. Cx22: A New Publicly Available Dataset for Deep Learning-Based Segmentation of Cervical Cytology Images (2022)

**Authors:** Guangqi Liu et al.

**Journal:** Computers in Biology and Medicine (2022)

This paper introduces the Cx22 dataset and discusses its use for semantic and instance segmentation of cervical cytology images.

**Paper:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compbiomed.2022.106194

## 2. Automated Cervical Cell Segmentation Using Deep Ensemble Learning (2023)

**Authors:** Jie Ji et al.

**Journal:** BMC Medical Imaging (2023)

This research focuses on automated segmentation of cervical cells, including cytoplasm and nucleus segmentation, using deep learning methods.

**Paper:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s12880-023-01096-1

## 3. Evaluation of the Cytonucleomorphometric Parameters for Cases Diagnosed as Squamous Cell Abnormality on Conventional Cervico-Vaginal Pap Smears (2023)

**Journal:** Journal of Cytology (2023)

This paper discusses cytonucleomorphometric parameters and their relationship with different cervical cytological categories.

It was referred to for understanding morphological measurements, particularly the nucleus-to-cytoplasm ratio.

**Paper:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10167830/

## 4. U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation (2015)

**Authors:** Olaf Ronneberger, Philipp Fischer and Thomas Brox.

**Conference:** MICCAI 2015

This paper introduces the U-Net architecture, which is widely used for biomedical image segmentation.

It was referred to for understanding the architecture used in our project.

**Paper:** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-24574-4_28
